Our Origin & Journey

Born From a Refusal to Accept Mediocre Software

In 2014, Nexa Agency was founded on a simple conviction: modern businesses deserve software that is visually stunning, technologically immaculate, and built to scale effortlessly.

Tired of bloated codebases, fragile architectures, and rigid agency hierarchies, our founders set out to build an agile engineering partner where senior talent works directly with clients to solve hard problems.

Over the past decade, we have expanded from a boutique San Francisco studio to a globally recognized digital agency. We have guided early-stage founders to multi-million dollar acquisitions, modernized legacy enterprises, and deployed digital platforms used by millions of active daily users.
